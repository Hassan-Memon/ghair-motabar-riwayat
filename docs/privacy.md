# Privacy policy — Tehqeeqat-e-Hadees apps

Applies to the Android, iOS and desktop apps that read [tehqeqatehadees.com](https://tehqeqatehadees.com) offline.

**The apps collect nothing.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

- **On your device only**: bookmarks, reading history, recent searches and settings are stored in a
  database on the device. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Network use**: the app downloads the latest research collection from GitHub Releases
  (`github.com`), at most once a day, on Wi-Fi unless you allow mobile data. These are ordinary file
  downloads; no information about you or your reading is sent. Opening a PDF or "open on website"
  loads that page in a browser.
- **Permissions**: internet access only.

Questions: open an issue on the project's repository or use the contact number published on the website.
